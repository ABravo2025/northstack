Northstack Cookie Policy

Effective Date: October 25, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains which cookies and similar technologies Northstack ("Northstack," "we," "us") uses on our public website (joinnorthstack.com) and in the Northstack application (app.joinnorthstack.com), what they are for, and how you can control them. It complements our Privacy Policy, in particular Section 2.4.

1. What cookies and local storage are

Cookies are small text files a website stores in your browser. Local storage is a similar browser feature that keeps small pieces of data on your device. We use both only as described below.

2. On our public website (joinnorthstack.com)

2.1 Strictly necessary

These are always on, because the site needs them to respect your choices.

NameTypePurposeDuration
ns-consentLocal storage (first party)Remembers whether you accepted or declined analytics cookies, so we don't ask again on every page.Until you clear your browser data or change your choice

2.2 Analytics (only if you accept)

We use Google Analytics, loaded through Google Tag Manager, to understand how visitors find and use the site (pages visited, the site you came from, approximate location derived from your IP address, device and browser type). These cookies are set only if you choose Accept in the cookie banner.

NameProviderPurposeDuration
_gaGoogle AnalyticsDistinguishes one browser from another to count unique visitors.2 years
_ga_DQJ1K0FVJ5Google AnalyticsKeeps the state of your current visit (session) on our site.2 years

Google Tag Manager itself is loaded on every page to read your choice; it does not set cookies. We do not enable Google Signals, cross-device tracking, or any advertising or remarketing feature, and advertising storage is always disabled.

2.3 Advertising cookies

We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

3. In the Northstack application (app.joinnorthstack.com)

The application does not use cookies. It keeps a small amount of data in your browser's local storage that it needs to work: your sign-in session (so you stay signed in) and preferences such as dark or light mode. This data stays on your device.

The application does not use Google Analytics yet. As described in Section 2.4 of our Privacy Policy, we plan to add usage analytics to the application; when we do, we will update this policy and post a new effective date.

4. How to control cookies

  • On our website: use the cookie banner, or the Cookie settings link at the bottom of every page, to accept or decline analytics cookies at any time. If you decline after having accepted, we delete the Google Analytics cookies from your browser.
    Open cookie settings
  • In your browser: you can block or delete cookies and local storage from your browser settings. Blocking local storage in the application will sign you out.
  • Google's tools: you can also install the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

5. Changes to this policy

If we change which cookies or similar technologies we use, we will update this page and its effective date.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy: info@joinnorthstack.com.
