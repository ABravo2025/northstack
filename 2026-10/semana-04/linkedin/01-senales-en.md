Spreadsheets don't break overnight. They break a little at a time.

5 signs your team has already outgrown them:

1️⃣ There are three versions of the same file and nobody knows which one is right.

2️⃣ To find out how many vacation days someone has left, you have to ask one specific person (who is, of course, on vacation).

3️⃣ Sales deals live in each rep's inbox. When someone leaves, the deals leave with them.

4️⃣ Adding a new hire means typing their details into four different places.

5️⃣ Everyone can see everything, salaries included.

If you checked two or more, it isn't a tidiness problem. Your team grew and your tools didn't.
