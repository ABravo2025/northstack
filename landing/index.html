FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every plan starts with 15 days of full access, and you don't need a credit card to begin.

Can I change plans later?

Anytime. Upgrade, downgrade or cancel from your billing settings; changes apply on your next billing cycle.

Is my data kept separate from other companies?

Yes. Every company's data is isolated at the database level and never shared with another workspace.

What happens when my trial ends?

You'll be asked to choose a plan and add your billing details. If you don't, your account moves into a grace period before access is limited.

Does Northstack pay my employees?

No. Payroll in Northstack is a record of compensation, pay runs and pay stubs. Northstack never processes or sends any payment; you keep paying your team through your own bank or payroll provider.
