Not everyone on your team needs to see salaries.

Sounds obvious. But at many small businesses, people data lives in a shared spreadsheet: either everyone can see it, or one person owns it and nobody else can help.

Neither one scales.

In Northstack, every role sees and does only what it should:

🔐 Create the roles your company actually needs (not just "admin" and "user").
✅ Turn on permissions one by one: who manages payroll, who decides time off, who sees dashboards, who invites people.
🧾 Payroll is visible only to those with that permission.
👤 Everyone sees their own data without access to anyone else's.

So your head of sales approves their team's time off without seeing the whole company's salaries. And HR stops being the only one who can answer everything.
