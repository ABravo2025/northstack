A new hire's first day says a lot about your company.

And it almost always goes wrong for the same reason: nobody knew they were supposed to do something.

Here's the checklist we use as a starting point, by stage:

📝 Before day one
• Add their details and contract
• Set up their accounts (email, tools)
• Get their equipment ready
• Tell the team who's joining and when

👋 Day 1
• Welcome and a walk around
• Introduce their manager and their team
• Show them how to request time off and who to ask about what

📅 Week 1
• First real tasks, small and achievable
• A short check-in at the end of the week: what's missing?

🎯 Month 1
• Clear goals for the next few months
• An honest conversation about how it's going

The trick isn't the list. It's that every task has an owner and a date.

In Northstack, this onboarding is a template: open it for each new hire and the tasks go straight to the right people (HR, IT, their manager).

What would you add to the list? 👇
