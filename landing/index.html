About the company

A true multi-tenant platform, from day one.

Every company on Northstack gets its own space, with full control over roles, permissions, and custom fields. It's modular from day one — built to grow alongside your team instead of forcing your operation into a rigid system.

AB
From the founder
Alejandro Bravo — Founder of Northstack

Throughout my career, I kept seeing the same problem repeat itself across different startups: HR, client management, and billing patched together with loose spreadsheets and tools that don't talk to each other. Northstack was born from that frustration — a platform built so every company can run itself independently, without bending its operations to fit a rigid system.

I'm actively building it today, focused on solid foundations before rushed features.
