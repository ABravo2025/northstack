FAQ

Questions about projects.

Which templates are included?

Templates for agencies (website, monthly campaign), accounting firms (monthly close, annual return, new client setup), consultancies (implementation, client onboarding) and internal projects. You can also start blank.

Can I create my own templates?

Yes, on the Growth plan. From any project choose “Save as template” and its phases and tasks are copied.

How many projects can I have?

Up to 5 open projects at a time on Starter; completed, cancelled and archived ones don’t count. Unlimited on Growth.

Can I link a project to a client?

Yes. Each project can belong to a company from your CRM or be internal, and each company lists its projects.

Can I track time per project?

Yes. Timesheet hours are logged per project, and reports show them by project or by client. Growth adds hour budgets and client reports.
