## 1. Novedad (tipo: Novedad / feature_update)

### Inglés

**Title:** New: Clients, Expenses, and Forms & surveys

**Summary:** Follow every client from lead to renewal, keep your company's expenses with an encrypted record, and collect answers with forms for your website and NPS/eNPS surveys. Available on Growth.

**Body:**

Three new areas, available on the Growth plan:

Clients (Sales)
- Leads: a new inbox with a 5-touch cadence, a 4-question qualification, and one-click conversion into company, contact, and opportunity.
- Pipeline rules: each stage can have a checklist to move forward and automations (create a task, send a survey). Your pipelines stay fully customizable.
- Quotes from your product catalog, with a private link your client accepts online; accepting moves the deal to Won.
- Handoff to the account owner on won deals: onboarding project, kickoff meeting, and welcome email.
- Client 360: Products, Timeline, Onboarding, Health score with check-ins, Tickets with SLA, and Profitability.
- Expansion & renewals: healthy clients with products they don't have, upcoming renewals, and clients at risk.

Expenses (Finance)
- Accounts, cards, and fixed expenses that book themselves on their charge day.
- Import the CSV statement from your bank or card: the file is read in your browser, never uploaded. Duplicates, transfers, installments, and dollar charges are handled for you.
- Budgets per category, subscription and price-increase alerts, receipts, and a 13-week cash projection. Amounts and descriptions are stored encrypted.

Forms & surveys
- Forms connected to a module (Leads, Clients, Employees, Support, Projects) that you can embed on your website.
- Client and employee surveys with personal links and automatic triggers. Employee surveys are anonymous by default.
- New dashboards: Surveys (NPS, CSAT, eNPS), Clients, and Expenses.

The User Guide (Help → User Guide) explains each one step by step.

### Español

**Título:** Nuevo: Clientes, Gastos y Formularios y encuestas

**Resumen:** Seguí a cada cliente desde que es lead hasta que renueva, llevá los gastos de tu empresa con registro cifrado, y juntá respuestas con formularios para tu web y encuestas NPS/eNPS. Disponible en Growth.

**Cuerpo:**

Tres áreas nuevas, disponibles en el plan Growth:

Clientes (Ventas)
- Leads: una bandeja nueva con cadencia de 5 toques, calificación de 4 preguntas y conversión en un clic a empresa, contacto y oportunidad.
- Reglas de pipeline: cada etapa puede tener un checklist para avanzar y automatizaciones (crear una tarea, enviar una encuesta). Tus pipelines siguen siendo totalmente configurables.
- Presupuestos desde tu catálogo de productos, con un link privado que tu cliente acepta online; al aceptarlo, el negocio pasa a Ganado.
- Traspaso al responsable de cuenta en los negocios ganados: proyecto de onboarding, reunión de inicio y mail de bienvenida.
- Ficha 360 del cliente: Productos, Línea de tiempo, Onboarding, Salud con check-ins, Tickets con SLA y Rentabilidad.
- Expansión y renovaciones: clientes sanos con productos que no tienen, renovaciones próximas y clientes en riesgo.

Gastos (Finanzas)
- Cuentas, tarjetas y gastos fijos que se registran solos el día del cobro.
- Importá el resumen CSV de tu banco o tarjeta: el archivo se lee en tu navegador y nunca se sube. Duplicados, transferencias, cuotas y cargos en dólares se resuelven solos.
- Presupuestos por categoría, avisos de suscripciones y aumentos, comprobantes y una proyección de caja a 13 semanas. Los montos y descripciones se guardan cifrados.

Formularios y encuestas
- Formularios conectados a un módulo (Leads, Clientes, Empleados, Soporte, Proyectos) que podés insertar en tu web.
- Encuestas a clientes y empleados con links personales y envíos automáticos. Las encuestas a empleados son anónimas por defecto.
- Dashboards nuevos: Encuestas (NPS, CSAT, eNPS), Clientes y Gastos.

La Guía del usuario (Ayuda → Guía del usuario) explica cada una paso a paso.

### Inglés

**Title:** We updated our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service

**Summary:** We added how we handle data in the new Clients, Expenses, and Forms & surveys modules, including the people who answer your forms and surveys. Effective November 2, 2026.

**Body:**

We updated our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service for the new Clients, Expenses, and Forms & surveys modules.

Privacy Policy
- What data these modules store, and which of it is encrypted with its own key (expenses, receipts, and form and survey answers).
- A new section about the people who answer your forms and surveys or accept a quote: what we record, how personal links work, and how anonymous surveys stay anonymous.
- Who can see expenses, profitability, and survey answers inside your company.
- Upstash, our provider for temporary abuse-protection counters, is now listed among our providers.

Terms of Service
- Expenses is a record-keeping tool: we don't connect to your bank or move money, and its numbers are estimates, not accounting advice.
- If you publish forms or send surveys, you're responsible for telling the people who answer how you use their answers.
- Accepting a quote online is a record of your client's response, not an electronic signature.

You can read both documents in full from Help → Legal.
