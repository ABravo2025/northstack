How it works

From request to approval, without chasing anyone.

1

Set up your policies

Vacation, sick leave, study days or whatever you use: days per year, paid or unpaid, whether it needs approval, and whether days arrive all at once or month by month.

2

Your team requests days

Each person picks the policy and the dates, and sees how many days they'll have left before sending.

3

Their manager approves

The request goes to their direct manager, who approves or rejects it with a reason. Both get notified in the app.

Holidays & business days

Days are counted the way your company counts them.

Choose business days or calendar days per policy. With business days, weekends, public holidays and company days off are never deducted.
